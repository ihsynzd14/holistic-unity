Rules (do not break these)

  • Keep the link dofollow — do NOT add rel="nofollow". The whole SEO value is the followed backlink. (rel="noopener" is fine and does not affect SEO.)
  • Each operator links to their own /t/<slug> profile — varied target URLs look natural to Google.
  • The utm_* params let you measure referral traffic in GA4 (Acquisition → operator-badge). They do not affect the backlink value (Google ignores utm for ranking).
  • Multilingual label: IT “Operatore Verificato”, EN “Verified Practitioner”, PT “Operador Verificado”.
