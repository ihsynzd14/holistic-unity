What is Quantum Touch Releasing® (QTR®)?

Quantum Touch Releasing®, often shortened to QTR®, is a vibrational, meditative energy technique founded by Ileana Rotella. According to QTR Academy, it brings together principles of quantum physics and sacred geometry with a practice of intention, symbols and words, with the aim of reawakening wellbeing and making it simpler to orient yourself toward your goals across the different areas of life.

QTR® works through particular vibrational “notes” made of symbols and words, guided by a specific intention. In the language of the technique, these notes are used to bring attention to personal energetic blocks — or ones inherited through the family tree: patterns that can keep a person caught in dynamics of struggle, low self-worth or loss of confidence.

It is not presented as an alternative therapy in the conventional sense, nor as a generic spiritual path. QTR Academy describes it as a tool for transformation that simplifies life, working on the deeper frequencies of identity — where, in this view, blocks are thought to originate before they show up in the body, the mind or everyday choices.

On Holistic Unity we present it as a bio-natural, meditative and experiential practice: a space to listen to what keeps repeating, to recognise the roots of a block, and to open a more conscious inner movement — without promises, and without replacing medical or psychological care.

How a QTR® Session Works

1

Intention & Listening

Share the theme you'd like to explore: a block, a fear, a choice, a relationship, or a feeling that keeps coming back into your life.

2

Meditative Space

The practitioner guides you into a state of greater calm and presence, creating the conditions to observe the theme without forcing it, judging it, or over-rationalising.

3

Working with Symbols & Frequencies

The technique uses particular vibrational notes made of symbols and words, guided by a specific intention. These elements are used as tools for awareness and energetic transformation.

4

Integration

The session closes with a moment of return and integration. You can take away new insights, a sense of greater clarity, or simple cues to keep listening to yourself.

Every practitioner may have their own way of leading the session, but the basic structure stays focused on listening, presence, and energetic work on the theme the person brings.

What QTR® Can Help With

⚡

Observe Recurring Patterns

Bring attention to dynamics that repeat over time — self-sabotage, a sense of being stuck, difficulty in relationships, mistrust, low self-esteem, or struggling to make decisions.

🌀

A Symbolic, Energetic Language

The method uses symbols and intention as a language to reach what often can't be explained by the rational mind alone — supporting a fuller sense of the body, of presence, and of the subtle sensations tied to the theme you're exploring.

🌳

Explore Roots, Find Direction

In the QTR® view, some blocks may be connected to the family system or inherited patterns. The session offers a space to look at them with more awareness and — without promising immediate solutions — to sense more clearly where you are and which inner movement you want to make.

Who QTR® May Be For

A QTR® session may suit those drawn to energetic, meditative and symbolic work, who want to explore a personal theme without necessarily entering a long or purely mental narrative. It can speak to anyone who feels they keep repeating the same patterns, who is going through a transition, who wants to work on self-esteem and confidence, or who wishes to look at their path from a more intuitive and spiritual perspective.

When to pair it with other support — If you're going through clinical trauma, a psychological disorder, a medical condition, or a moment of strong emotional instability, QTR® should not be used as a substitute for psychotherapy, medical treatment or qualified healthcare. In these cases it may, at most, accompany them as a complementary practice — with good sense and alongside the right professionals.
